The [existing lifetime audit](w4-gather-lifetime-2b8.md) remains applicable outside the cutoff: raw SMEM, decoded TMEM and B SMEM have distinct owners; required empty/full edges and producer tails are not duplicates. Rubin/Mega differences do not provide a free replacement for these contracts. The [defined-zero M audit](w4-oob-m-zero-fill-reassessment.md) also remains closed: a private predicate/handoff and zero path cannot shorten the valid peer's transform merely by reducing invalid-CTA instruction count.

**Decision:** no prototype or new profiling request from this pass. The current alpha-prefetch experiment is swap-only and remains independent; W4 already uses that overlap pattern. A future W4 proposal needs a newly identified executed redundant operation or source-bound critical-path gap beyond these closed mechanisms.
