## What preferred4 changes

The swapped GEMM treats packed NVFP4 weights as A and BF16 activations as B. Extending the channel-axis cluster from two to four CTAs allows two M256/two-CTA MMA pairs to reuse the same activation tile. The B descriptors and multicast mask encode that sharing: [megamoe_kernel.py:903–920](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L903-L920) and [1509–1526](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L1509-L1526). This reduces duplicate activation requests across the preferred cluster. Equivalent HBM-byte savings are not established; repeated requests can be served by cache.

The added channel-axis sharing does not share packed weights or decoded BF16 weights between the two MMA pairs. Weight/SF slicing and the two TMA copies remain at [995–1051](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L995-L1051). Each CTA retains the two established decode warp groups at [1732–1744](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L1732-L1744), and the MMA path remains two-CTA at [1626–1647](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L1626-L1647). The mixed launch fills the cluster4 packing remainder with fallback2; uniform2 already launches all 148 CTAs on the measured B300. Packing alone therefore supplies no demonstrated advantage over uniform2 here.

## Remaining structural costs, without causal attribution

- **Descriptor selection is already hoisted.** Candidate2 moved selection of both FC executable atoms and the multicast mask outside the persistent work loop at [1509–1526](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L1509-L1526). The per-K call at [1153–1158](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L1153-L1158) passes `None` for descriptor/mask updates. The maintained [helper:62–75](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/kernel_src/sm100/next_cutedsl_megamoe/src/blackwell_genphase_sources/kernel_src/blackwell/custom_mix_cga_helpers.py#L62-L75) rebinds the current barrier. There is no repeated runtime descriptor selection left to remove.
- **Activation reuse couples reader completion.** With B-only dependency tracking, [helper:85–89 and 220–264](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/kernel_src/sm100/next_cutedsl_megamoe/src/blackwell_genphase_sources/kernel_src/blackwell/custom_mix_cga_helpers.py#L220-L264) sets two MMA-consumer arrivals for preferred4 versus one for fallback2. [Consumer release:308–310](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/kernel_src/sm100/next_cutedsl_megamoe/src/blackwell_genphase_sources/kernel_src/blackwell/custom_mix_cga_helpers.py#L308-L310) multicasts completion before producers reuse the stages. This is a required consequence of sharing data across the two MMA pairs, not a diagnosed bottleneck or removable redundant barrier.
- **Fallback groups exchange work IDs.** The established fixed-group protocol registers fallback clusters once at [work_id_claim.py:503–543](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/kernel_src/sm100/next_cutedsl_megamoe/src/blackwell_genphase_sources/kernel_src/schedulers/work_id_claim.py#L503-L543), then uses generation-token handoff at [573–615](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/kernel_src/sm100/next_cutedsl_megamoe/src/blackwell_genphase_sources/kernel_src/schedulers/work_id_claim.py#L573-L615). Candidate3r already overlaps registration with dispatch startup at [kernel:1423–1433](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/megamoe_kernel.py#L1423-L1433).
- **Work granularity grows.** Each preferred work ID covers twice the channel work; two fallback clusters jointly cover the same logical tile. See [frontend:240–248](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/frontend.py#L240-L248) and [scheduler:446–451](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/fc1_fc2_fuse_sched.py#L446-L451). This changes scheduling granularity and phase-group work. Mixed group256 was added to match uniform2/group512 channel work when tiles divide evenly. No further group or static-scheduling expansion is justified by this review.

These costs make a small or neutral net benefit structurally plausible when activation service is already overlapped. They do not identify the cause of any measured latency difference.

## What the PC capture establishes

The [candidate PC analysis](../evidence/remote/profiles/glm5-candidate3r-auto18-mixed-only-pc-t4096-a2/pc-analysis/ANALYSIS.md) preserves all per-rank samples and source/SASS audits. Both selected kernels report 96 registers/thread, 640 threads/block, 148 grid CTAs, and 222,592 dynamic shared bytes/block. Thus this selected profile does not show a larger register/shared-memory launch footprint. Static SASS grows from 6,392 to 7,080 instructions; that is code size, not executed instruction count or measured cost.

Startup readiness heavily skews the samples. The least-startup baseline/candidate ranks show 77/79 MMA activation-ready-wait samples and zero samples in the explicit activation-producer blocking stubs. Those counts are neither elapsed-time shares nor proof that waits never occur. Approximately 44% of candidate rank6 samples remain unclassified. Candidate profiling selected group512 while its cold-T4096 timing winner used group1024. The profile therefore cannot provide an exact-tactic explanation of that timing difference. It establishes actual preferred/fallback path execution on sampled ranks and does not establish an activation, raw-weight, conversion, or scheduler bottleneck.

## Conditional catalog recommendation

The 18 new tactics are a reasonable exploratory set: M256 × N32/N64/N128 × group256/512/1024 × the two existing return modes. They are not yet supported as default AUTO additions by retained performance evidence. [autotune.py:30–69](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/autotune.py#L30-L69) grows the default catalog from 16 to 34; with existing IKR opt-in it grows from 24 to 51. [The sweep:280–292](../flashinfer/flashinfer/moe_ep/cute_dsl/megamoe/bf16_nvfp4/autotune.py#L280-L292) prepares, compiles, warms, and captures every candidate. Additional tuning work is certain; its wall-clock increase is not inferred from candidate count.

Wait for the full13 comparison. If it finds no retained gains, preserve the original default AUTO catalog and keep mixed capability available through explicit knobs/candidate lists while qualification remains scoped separately. If gains are retained in a supported range, use those results to justify a bounded inclusion policy and complete the corresponding correctness checks before promotion. Do not infer a token threshold or prune to a single tactic from the five exploratory points. The existing explicit configuration still defaults to uniform2; this recommendation concerns the AUTO search catalog.

This note is analysis only. It introduces no product change, new experiment axis, GPU run, remote operation, or publication.
