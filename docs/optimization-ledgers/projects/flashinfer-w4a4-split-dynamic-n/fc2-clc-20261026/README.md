Candidate compiler artifacts use a verified source-private namespace; unchanged native artifacts are reused. No source or native timing cache is rewritten while running.

The earlier `scripts/fc2_clc_experiment` was based on `0c1e00f` without weight startup prefetch. It showed small V41 gains at T32/T128 and regressions for NV/mixed T512. The current test is motivated by new full-prefetch context and NSYS/NCU localization of K3's gap to exposed FC2, supported by V41 MXFP8 memory counters.

First screen is fixed replay of fresh native AUTO selections: K3 NV T64/T128, V41 MXFP8 T128, then K3 both MX formats T32/T64/T128/T512 and NV T32/T512. Four interleaved C–A–A–C arms on GPU6; 10 warmups, 100 cold-L2 CUPTI graph samples; NV initial quantization included and fused finalize off. Actual cache hits must equal the unchanged saved full-campaign cache entries; routes must equal the full campaign. Read-only constructor observation confirms effective FC2 CLC and PDL prefetch. Results are diagnostic, not native AUTO retention evidence. No new upstream tests.

## First completed CAAC evidence

K3 W4A4 improves 1.006412x at T64 and 1.010263x at T128; V41 MXFP8 T128 improves 1.009507x. K3 MXFP8 improves 1.005661–1.009612x at T32/T64/T128/T512; mixed improves 1.005022–1.006287x at T32/T64/T128. However, K3 mixed T512 regresses to 0.971001x in both paired comparisons. That selected native tactic has FC1 CLC disabled. These are measured fixed-tactic results, not evidence that all AUTO choices regress. The unconditional FC2 request is not retained.

Follow-up screens use unchanged source and native geometry: all three NV arms and both MX arms at T4096 for V3 (GPU1) and K3 (GPU3), plus per-token T32/T128. GPU6 finishes K3 NV T32/T512. Full-precision values, raw samples, observed constructor choices and native cache-hit receipts are in `evidence/`; `summary.json` is generated by `scripts/fc2_clc_20261026/summarize.py`.

Historical CLC depth2 was separately rejected for decode losses of 3–9%; see `scripts/clc_regular_tma_experiment/AUDIT.md`. This experiment preserves depth1, and does not add model/dtype thresholds or couple FC2 scheduling implicitly to FC1.

## Frozen screen outcome

All 10 screens (40 processes) completed. See `results.md` for all cells and paired ratios, and `summary.json` for full precision. The unconditional policy is rejected. Prefill regressions are consistent: V3 W4A4/per-token/mixed are 0.98121/0.98894/0.98228x; K3 W4A4/per-token/mixed/MXFP8 are 0.97010/0.97915/0.96699/0.99552x. V3 MXFP8 is neutral. K3 per-token decode T32/T128 improves 1.00261/1.00804x; V3 per-token decode is small/neutral.

The next source-paired gate is prepared locally in `scripts/fc2_clc_20261026/probe_scheduler_pair.py` and `run_pair_gate.py`. It waits for the independent scheduler-AUTO candidate source receipt. No numerical correctness claim is made for this rejected constant-CLC screen.
