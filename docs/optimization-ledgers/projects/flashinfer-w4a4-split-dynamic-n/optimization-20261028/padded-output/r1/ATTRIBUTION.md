Independent raw performance audit passes 2,037 artifact hashes, 348 source files per role, 15 jobs and physical GPUs, 144 cells, 576 medians, and 57,600 strict CUPTI samples. The 112 scaled cells are the affected cohort; 32 W4A16 cells remain a separate unchanged-path sanity arm. All winners, route identities and managed-cache contents match the retained baseline. The initial observer startup failure produced no measurements and is excluded.

Scaled geomean speedups: EP decode −0.533%, EP prefill +0.481%; TP decode −2.655%, TP prefill +3.967%. These are the standard 100-sample medians. They do not justify ignoring individual decode losses.

The largest TP decode loss is N3U TP8, 4 tokens, MXFP4x8: control passes 36.320/36.352 us, candidate 40.2875/40.208 us, −9.719%. It uses FC2 M128/N16, one CTA and ordinary scheduling. This is the nonbulk SIMT path, whose gate instruction counts shrink, so the duplicated bulk-tail epilogue is not its cause. FC2 output allocation changes from 88×2048 BF16 (0.34375 MiB) to 1408×2048 (5.5 MiB). Only valid rows are read and written; allocation growth is not equivalent to 16× measured traffic. The new row spacing and inverse-mapped gather, compared with compact expanded output, are source-level candidates for the component attribution.

For this 4-token/H2048 case, combine geometry is unchanged: 32 threads, one vector/thread, eight hidden tiles/token. Both layouts consume the same 22 ordered weighted slots. The source difference is dynamic mapped-row addressing versus expanded `token*top_k+slot`, and the changed location of valid output rows. There is no evidence yet assigning the measured ~3.9 us increase to combine, FC2 or their PDL overlap. Component timing/profile evidence is needed before selecting a remedy.

The separate bulk-tail codegen expansion is real: normal bulk64 N128 adds310–421 static non-NOP sites; bulk64 N256 adds1674–2561; forced bulk32 N256 adds1366–1422. The normal N256 overlap walk inlines16 copies of the TMEM-transpose/SIMT tail body across its two directions. This is static duplication, not16 executed tails per tile. All compared operand/accumulator/native-MMA geometry and stage plans match. Resource and opcode details are bound in `../codegen-growth-review.json`; no blanket instruction-reduction claim is appropriate for r1.
