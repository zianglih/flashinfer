# W4A16 batched routes: duplicate review

The new7739 candidate is the same lane-batching mechanism rejected earlier in `../../w4a16-batched-route-ids/RESULTS.md`. `duplicate-proof.json` reconstructs the earlier kernel from6c plus its saved patch and verifies its exact86f7d34b hash. The entire gather branch AST matches the new8f6cbafd source after ignoring only the placement of one compile-time constant assignment. Intervening W4 changes are the independent plain weight-acquire/MMA-wait cleanups; they do not change gather ownership or establish a new batching mechanism. No new whole-kernel binary identity is claimed.

For group batch b, power-of-two L≤8, and requested cache group i<L / coordinate j<4, the shuffle selects lane4i+j. Its decoded group is bL+((lane>>2)&(L−1)) = bL+i; its low lane bits are j. Thus the selected row is exactly the old `(row_lane+(bL+i)*row_issuers)*4+j`. Global CTA thread IDs add multiples of8 after shifting, which are removed by the L−1 mask. Every coordinate remains warp-uniform after the same SHFL.

The final partial batch copies only its actual group count. Extra producer lanes with group≥groups_per_warp have row≥rows and cannot load route memory. The `row<rows` and `row+row_shift<valid` predicates, UInt32 topK division, −1 sentinel, dynamic two-CTA offset, work-tile loop and K payload loop are unchanged. In particular, N16 with one or two CTAs folds to one group/load and preserves its narrow row/K issuer split. Existing345,600-case proof covers legal N8..256, both CTA counts, all valid tails, static/dynamic offsets and topK1/2/3/8/22; its binding is included in the receipt.

The previous33+4 exact cases and no-local codegen already proved safety and instruction reduction: N128 two-CTA gather275→255 instructions /4→1 route-load sites; N192344→321 /6→1. N16 CUBIN was identical. That candidate was rejected after unresolved V41F EP4K−1.830% and EP16K−4.072%; raw variation and negatives remain in the old report. This rediscovery should not launch another CAAC without a new mechanism or explicit reconsideration.
